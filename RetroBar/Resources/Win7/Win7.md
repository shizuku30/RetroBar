This folder contains additional files for the Windows 7 XAML theme

`InactivePointerOver.png`, `ActivePointerOver.png`, `notRunningPointerOver.png`
and `notRunningPressed.png` are the complete 60x40 states 3, 6, 9 and 10,
respectively, of `IMAGE/1014` from the supplied Windows 7
`aero.msstyles` 6.1.7600.16385. Source SHA-256:
`6da5ef369e56d81ba737b65d69a3316d40096e52b1a1bedb87a90f5dbf6925e2`.
The resource's premultiplied RGB was converted to straight alpha for PNG/WPF;
the original transparent top rows are retained rather than cropped away.
